DEPARTMENT OF FISH — SECURE WATERS
FORTINET NSE 7 - FORTISASE 26 ARCHITECT
40-EPISODE STORY STUDY PLAN

FORMAT
- Six seasons
- 20–30 minutes per episode
- One or two connected mental models
- Persistent topology
- Gradual-evidence troubleshooting
- Optional mini-labs
- SD-WAN knowledge diagnosed and compressed when strong
- FortiSASE taught mainly through user journeys

SEASON 1 — THE GLASS WALL
Zoo HQ launches a hybrid-access programme while the fish watch from the aquarium wall.

EP-01 — The Office Behind the Ocean
SASE, SSE, SD-WAN, unified SASE, SIA, SPA, SSA.
Problem: inspectors, researchers, and contractors all use different access paths.
Mental model: SIA protects internet; SPA protects private apps; SSA governs SaaS.

EP-02 — The User’s Journey
Endpoint, identity, steering, POP, policy, destination, visibility.
Problem: where is trust evaluated, and where can failure occur?

EP-03 — Which POP?
POP selection, latency, availability, path versus apparent geography.
Problem: identical policy, different user experience.

EP-04 — The Address Everyone Trusts
Dedicated public IP, SaaS allowlisting, geolocation tag, source IP anchoring.
Problem: a government SaaS service blocks shared egress.

EP-05 — The Licence Tank
Licences, FortiFlex, provisioning, MSSP workflow.
Problem: procurement asks what is architectural and what is licence-dependent.

EP-06 — Thin Edge, Thick Edge
FortiAP, FortiExtender/FortiBranch, FortiGate branch, private proxy.
Problem: Fish needs a tiny field site without local security staff.

EP-07 — Season Incident: The Vanishing Researcher
Integrated user-journey troubleshooting.
Problem: SaaS works but a private application does not.
Season review: outside coffee near the kangaroos.

SEASON 2 — OPEN WATER
The Department expands secure internet access for remote and edge users.

EP-08 — Agent on Board
FortiClient agent-based SIA, installer, tunnel, steering, authentication source.

EP-09 — No Agent, Still Secure
Agentless SIA, use cases, limitations, trade-offs.

EP-10 — Edge Devices into SIA
SIA for edge devices, FortiAP, FortiExtender/FortiBranch, thin-edge onboarding.

EP-11 — Branch On-Ramp
Branch on-ramp, SD-WAN on-ramp, site-based users, local versus cloud security.

EP-12 — The Content Filter
SWG, FWaaS, RBI, secure browser extension, inspection choices.

EP-13 — SaaS in the Shadows
SSA, CASB, DLP, inline versus API visibility, data at rest and in motion.

EP-14 — The Slow Current
DEM, SaaS monitoring, endpoint, LAN, network path, and POP evidence.

EP-15 — Season Incident: The Clownfish Outage
SIA troubleshooting with POP status, FortiClient diagnostics, DEM, and packet path.
Season review: outside coffee near the kangaroos.

SEASON 3 — PRIVATE REEFS
The Department replaces broad network access with explicit private application access.

EP-16 — Private Does Not Mean Trusted
SPA, ZTNA, explicit application access, use cases.

EP-17 — The Access Proxy
FortiGate ZTNA access proxy, SPA FortiGate configuration, certificates, publishing.

EP-18 — SPA Through SD-WAN
SPA using SD-WAN, single and dual hub, FortiSASE as a spoke.

EP-19 — Agentless ZTNA
Proxy SSO, browser access, unmanaged endpoints, limits.

EP-20 — Posture Before Access
Endpoint profiles, posture tags, compliance rules, endpoint upgrades.

EP-21 — On-Net, Off-Net
Network lockdown, steering bypass, on-net/off-net behaviour.

EP-22 — Experience Is Evidence
DEM for SPA, endpoint path monitoring, traceroute, TCP latency.

EP-23 — Season Incident: The Locked Aquarium
Integrated SPA, posture, identity, tunnel, and private-application troubleshooting.
Season review: outside coffee near the kangaroos.

SEASON 4 — THE CURRENT BENEATH
The Department scales SD-WAN and integrates it tightly with FortiSASE.

EP-24 — Up Is Not Good
Members, zones, underlay, overlay, active/passive monitoring, SLA targets.
Mental model: up is a fact; good is a measurement.

EP-25 — The First Packet Chooses
Rule lookup, explicit and implicit rules, local-out, session state.

EP-26 — Who Wins?
Manual, best quality, lowest cost, load balance, member priority.

EP-27 — Routes Beneath Rules
Policy routes, route lookup, member/zone/probe routes, session flags, reevaluation, SNAT route changes.

EP-28 — Application Learning
Application steering, learning phases, ISDB destination, rule status.

EP-29 — Zero-Touch Field Sites
ZTP, blueprints, CSV imports, branch deployment workflow.

EP-30 — Overlay Orchestration
FortiManager SD-WAN Manager, prerequisites, IPsec, BGP, ADVPN templates.

EP-31 — Two Hubs, One Department
Tuggers and Gunners, primary/secondary versus primary/primary, hub cost, scale.

EP-32 — Season Incident: The Wrong Hub
Dual-hub routing, SLA, rules, sessions, FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer evidence.
Season review: outside coffee near the kangaroos.

SEASON 5 — SHORTCUTS AND SPOKES
The overlay must scale, self-heal, and carry private access.

EP-33 — BGP Per Overlay
IPsec and BGP per overlay, address planning, additional paths, scalability.

EP-34 — BGP on Loopback
Loopback peering, session scaling, overlay stickiness, design comparison.

EP-35 — Self-Healing Routes
BGP self-healing, route-map-out-preferable, minimum SLA members, remote health concepts.

EP-36 — Shortcut Through the Reef
ADVPN, shortcut negotiation, spoke-to-spoke traffic.

EP-37 — FortiSASE as a Spoke
Security POPs as spokes, remote-user private access, SD-WAN integration.

EP-38 — Season Incident: The Shortcut That Never Formed
ADVPN, BGP, IPsec, routing, and SPA impact.
Season review: outside coffee near the kangaroos.

SEASON 6 — GLOBAL WATERS
The Department expands globally and must operate FortiSASE and SD-WAN as one architecture.

EP-39 — Multiregion and Sovereignty
Multiregion, regional routing, VRF-aware overlays, data residency, data sovereignty, MSSP use cases.

EP-40 — The Secure Waters Incident
Integrated final incident:
- FortiSASE provisioning
- SIA, SPA, SSA
- endpoint posture
- SD-WAN steering
- IPsec and ADVPN
- FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer
- SOCaaS and forensics
- FortiClient diagnostics
- FortiSASE packet capture

Final scene:
Samwise presents in the Arowana Boardroom, then joins Señor Gonzalez outside near the kangaroos for coffee while avoiding the landmines.

FINAL REVIEW
Weight the final question bank by:
- blueprint percentages
- knowledge-register weaknesses
- hint dependency
- integrated user journeys
- troubleshooting evidence
