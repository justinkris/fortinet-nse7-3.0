DEPARTMENT OF FISH — SASE STORY MODE PACKAGE
============================================

Document Version: 2.0
Last Updated: 31 July 2026
Certification Path:
• Fortinet NSE 5 — FortiSASE 26 Core Administrator
• Fortinet NSE 7 — FortiSASE 26 Architect

PURPOSE
-------
This package turns the FortiSASE certification path into one continuous
Department of Fish technical story.

The learner begins by operating a small FortiSASE deployment for the Department's
new MYBAD public contact centre. The environment then grows into a distributed,
multi-region SASE and SD-WAN architecture involving remote workers, branches,
private applications, cloud services, FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer.

STAGE BOUNDARY
--------------
Seasons 1–3:
    NSE 5 FortiSASE Core Administrator

Seasons 4–6:
    NSE 7 FortiSASE Architect
