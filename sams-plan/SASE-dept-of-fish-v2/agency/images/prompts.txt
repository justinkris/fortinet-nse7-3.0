FortiSASE · Department of Fish — Locations Image Prompts
==============================================================================

STYLE:
  Simple, clean, editorial-style cartoon illustration. Bold black linework on an off-white cream paper background. Deliberately restrained palette: black outlines plus at most TWO muted flat accent colours per illustration (choose from muted rose, dusty sage, warm ochre, faded rust, or slate grey — never bright, never neon, never rainbow). Most of the drawing is line art with only small areas of flat colour used as accent. Detailed but uncluttered: characters, key props and one clear focal action are recognisable; no busy backgrounds, no gradients, no shading, no cross-hatching, no photorealism. Confident single-weight ink lines. 16:9 landscape composition. Slight government-office comedy tone, but the technical elements should still be recognisable. No vendor logos, no readable brand names on hardware.
