FortiSASE · Department of Fish — Image Prompts
==============================================================================

One prompt per SASE episode. Each PNG is referenced by study-plan/index.html.

STYLE (applies to every image):
  Simple, clean, editorial-style cartoon illustration. Bold black linework on an off-white cream paper background. Deliberately restrained palette: black outlines plus at most TWO muted flat accent colours per illustration (choose from muted rose, dusty sage, warm ochre, faded rust, or slate grey — never bright, never neon, never rainbow). Most of the drawing is line art with only small areas of flat colour used as accent. Detailed but uncluttered: characters, key props and one clear focal action are recognisable; no busy backgrounds, no gradients, no shading, no cross-hatching, no photorealism. Confident single-weight ink lines. 16:9 landscape composition. Slight government-office comedy tone, but the technical elements should still be recognisable. No vendor logos, no readable brand names on hardware.

CAST REFERENCE (draw whoever is named in the SCENE below):
  Samwise — thoughtful mid-30s Australian network and security engineer, patterned button-down shirt, laptop with stickers, slightly wry expression.
  Señor Gonzalez — distinguished 50-something senior engineer, wire-frame glasses, often holding a mandarin, burrito or Corona with a lemon twist. Gently amused.
  Junior — enthusiastic early-20s junior engineer, hoodie under a Zoo lanyard, tablet with stylus, jumping ahead of the evidence.
  Dr Eleanor — composed early-50s security architect and incident specialist, tailored dark blazer, plain black notebook, evidence-driven expression.
  Fysh — the unseen Head of Department. Only appears as a Microsoft Teams tile with a generic fish silhouette profile image, camera off, mic off, status 'Available'.
  Baz — operations analyst who trusts green dashboards too quickly.
  Penny — service-desk lead with sharp user symptoms.
  Riley — platform engineer with deployment and automation context.
  The Fish Guy — an unnamed supporting character who appears briefly and is never fully explained.

SETTING REFERENCE:
  ZOO HQ is a small government office tucked into the National Zoo and Aquarium in Canberra, home to the Department of Fish. Backdrop is a large glowing aquarium wall behind desks. Clown Fish Coffee is inside; Roo Coffee is outside. Meeting rooms are named after exotic fish (Dragonet, Clown Triggerfish, Arowana Boardroom, Mandarin Fish, Blue Tang). The MYBAD Operations Centre is a chaotic public contact centre. Tuggers Data Centre (Tuggeranong) and Gunners Data Centre (Gungahlin) host the private applications and SD-WAN hubs.
