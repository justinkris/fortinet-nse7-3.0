STYLE (applies to every portrait below):
Flat cartoon character portrait. Detailed but visually simple. Warm
cream-and-navy palette on a cream background (#faf5e9). Clean thin
outlines, solid flat colour fills, gentle flat shading, expressive but
uncluttered faces. No gradients, no photorealism, no painterly texture,
no lens blur, no noise. Character framed roughly head-to-waist and
centred. No text on signage, no vendor logos, no watermarks.

Aspect ratio: 3:4 portrait (taller than wide, e.g. 900 x 1200 px).
Compose the character vertically so the frame reads well when cropped
tall — head and shoulders emphasised, useful negative space above and
below.
