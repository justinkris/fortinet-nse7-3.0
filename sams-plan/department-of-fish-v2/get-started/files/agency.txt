DEPARTMENT OF FISH
AGENCY REGISTER — VERSION 2.0

STARTING STATE

- Telstra Tower, Canberra: Department of Fish main office
- Tuggers: primary data centre
- Gunners: secondary data centre
- Fibre between Tuggers and Gunners
- The main office was deliberately placed at Telstra Tower because it is centrally located between the two data centres
- FortiGate HA clusters
- FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer
- Two WAN links at Tuggers
- Basic SD-WAN
- Small VLAN estate
- Simple routing
- Operational, but not standardised for national scale

GROWTH PATH

1. Mature basic SD-WAN at Tuggers
2. Add Sydney and Brisbane
3. Convert Tuggers single hub into a Tuggers/Gunners dual-hub design
4. Standardise ZTP and FortiManager orchestration
5. Expand to Melbourne, Perth, and Darwin
6. Introduce ADVPN and self-healing
7. Build multiregion global operations

MAIN OFFICE

Location:
- Telstra Tower, Canberra

Role:
- Department headquarters
- Executive offices
- Network and security operations
- Main user floor
- Primary meeting and incident-response location

Design rationale:
- Chosen as a central headquarters between Tuggers and Gunners
- Gives the Department a neutral operational centre separate from either data centre
- Allows incidents to affect the office, Tuggers, or Gunners independently
- Creates a natural need for resilient connectivity from the office to both data centres

Story implications:
- Samwise, Señor Gonzalez, Junior, Mel, and Aisha are usually based here
- Tuggers and Gunners are visited for data-centre work, maintenance, and incidents
- The office should have resilient connectivity to both data centres
- Do not describe the office as physically attached to Tuggers
