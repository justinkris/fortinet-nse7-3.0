Story-Based Study Plan

Six seasons, thirty-six episodes. Every episode gives a Fortinet capability a concrete operational reason to exist — an incident, an audit, an expansion, or a design call Samwise has to make on the way to becoming the Department’s secure networking architect.

Purpose

Why this plan exists

This plan prepares Samwise, an experienced Fortinet administrator, for the Fortinet NSE 7 Secure Networking Architect exam. Each episode introduces a realistic operational need — an incident, project, audit, or design decision — that gives the technology a reason to exist.

  • SD-WAN — fundamentals, strategies, DIA
  • Central management & orchestration
  • OSPF, BGP, and routing at scale
  • Rules, sessions, and steering behaviour
  • Fabric, automation, and HA (FGCP / FGSP)
  • Advanced IPsec, ADVPN, and multiregion
Session Design

How each episode is run

  • Target duration: 20–30 minutes.
  • Story-led opening establishes the operational problem.
  • Socratic learning is the main phase — one question at a time.
  • Configuration only after the mental model is stable.
  • Troubleshooting reveals evidence gradually.
  • End with 5–10 exam questions and generate the two required artefacts.
Adaptive Session Rule

Diagnose first, then choose the mode

At the start of each episode, run a fast diagnostic through the story:

  • Ask Samwise to explain the purpose of the technology.
  • Ask one behaviour-prediction question.
  • Ask for one likely verification step.
COMPRESSStrong answers; move quickly to scenario and exam distinctions.
NORMALPartial understanding; use the standard episode.
REINFORCEWeak mental model; narrow the episode and build the foundation.
SKIP/REASSIGNDemonstrated mastery; record evidence and move the time to a weak area.

Do not raise confidence because an episode was completed. Raise it only when Samwise demonstrates understanding.
