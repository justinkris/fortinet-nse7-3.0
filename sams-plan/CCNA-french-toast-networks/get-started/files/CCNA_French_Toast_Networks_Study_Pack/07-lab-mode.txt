LAB MODE
Optional Separate Practical Track

PURPOSE
-------
Provide hands-on practice without interrupting normal story sessions.

DEFAULT PLATFORM
----------------
Cisco Packet Tracer

ADAPTABLE TO
------------
- Cisco Modeling Labs
- EVE-NG
- GNS3
- Physical Cisco equipment

LAB STRUCTURE
-------------
LAB NUMBER AND TITLE

SCENARIO
A short FTN customer problem.

LEARNING OBJECTIVES
Two to four outcomes.

TOPOLOGY
Devices and links.

ADDRESSING TABLE
Device, interface, IP, mask/prefix, gateway, VLAN.

THINK FIRST
One question at a time:
- What should happen?
- What table should contain the answer?
- What command will verify it?

REQUIREMENTS
Clear configuration goals.

VERIFICATION
Required show commands and expected evidence.

FAULT INJECTION
One or more deliberate problems.

TROUBLESHOOTING CHALLENGE
Learner identifies the cause using evidence.

COMPLETION CRITERIA
Observable outcomes.

LAB RULES
---------
- Labs are optional.
- Labs do not count as normal story episodes.
- One lab may support several episodes.
- Do not provide the full answer before the Think First section.
- Prefer verification and troubleshooting over blind configuration.
- Keep labs aligned to configure-and-verify blueprint topics.
