CCNA 200-301 STORY-MODE STUDY PACK
French Toast Networks

Purpose
-------
This pack defines a reusable story-driven CCNA learning program built around French Toast Networks, a small managed network services provider in France.

The learner follows Aussie, a recently arrived Australian network technician, as French Toast Networks grows from a tiny office above La Crêpe de Maman into a larger managed services provider.

Design principles
-----------------
- Short sessions: approximately 20–30 minutes
- Mostly Socratic teaching
- One clear question at a time
- Brief direct explanation when needed
- Story context without letting story overwhelm the technology
- Optional labs kept separate from teaching sessions
- Exam scope controlled by the official CCNA 200-301 v1.1 blueprint
- Reusable for another learner, with Aussie as the protagonist

Core outputs after each session
-------------------------------
- session-summary-ep-XX.txt
- knowledge-register-ep-XX.txt
- updated master mind map

Recommended usage
-----------------
1. Read 01-CCNA-master-study-plan.txt.
2. Review the organisation and character files.
3. Use 05-teaching-mode.txt for normal sessions.
4. Use 06-review-mode.txt for season reviews.
5. Use 07-lab-mode.txt only when a separate practical lab is wanted.
6. Update the knowledge register and mind map after every session.
7. Use the blueprint mapping CSV to confirm complete exam coverage.
