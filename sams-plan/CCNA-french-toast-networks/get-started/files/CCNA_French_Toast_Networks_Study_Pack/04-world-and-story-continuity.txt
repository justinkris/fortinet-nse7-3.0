WORLD AND STORY CONTINUITY
French Toast Networks

PURPOSE
-------
This file defines the stable story world and the rules for maintaining continuity across episodes.

CORE SETTING
------------
French Toast Networks operates from The Toast Rack, a small office above La Crêpe de Maman.

The office begins with:
- One small open workspace
- A whiteboard
- One equipment rack
- A meeting table
- A narrow open area where Rafa practises capoeira
- A stairway leading directly down to the crêpe shop

STORY TIMELINE
--------------
Time remains deliberately vague.

The company grows across several seasons, but no exact real-world year progression is required.

CUSTOMER PROGRESSION
--------------------
1. La Crêpe de Maman
2. Nearby cafés and small retailers
3. Small local office customers
4. Le School de Croissant
5. Second school campus
6. Multiple managed-services customers

NETWORK MATURITY PROGRESSION
----------------------------
Phase 1:
Small unmanaged or lightly managed networks

Phase 2:
Basic Cisco switching and routing standards

Phase 3:
Structured VLANs and campus switching

Phase 4:
Multi-site routing and OSPF

Phase 5:
Operational services and monitoring

Phase 6:
Security standards

Phase 7:
Controller-based and automated operations

CONTINUITY PRINCIPLES
---------------------
- Existing device names remain consistent.
- Existing subnets remain consistent unless a redesign is part of the story.
- Character expertise remains consistent.
- Previously solved faults should not reappear without a new cause.
- Customer requirements may evolve, but earlier decisions should be acknowledged.
- The learner’s strengths and weaknesses should be carried forward through the knowledge register.

STORY-TO-TECHNOLOGY RULE
------------------------
Every story problem must exist to support a technical objective.

Do not add:
- Long side plots
- Unrelated drama
- Character scenes that delay the lesson
- Humour that hides the packet path or exam point

LOCATION RULE
-------------
Each new location must include:
- Business purpose
- Users
- Devices
- Connectivity needs
- Security needs
- Operational constraints

DOCUMENTATION RULE
------------------
As FTN matures, documentation should visibly improve:
- Hostnames
- Interface descriptions
- VLAN IDs
- IP plans
- Diagrams
- Change notes
- Customer handover records

FOOTBALL BANTER RULE
--------------------
Football banter is recurring background humour only.

Use sparingly:
- France versus Brazil
- Aussie saying soccer
- Aussie explaining rugby league

Do not let it dominate technical scenes.
