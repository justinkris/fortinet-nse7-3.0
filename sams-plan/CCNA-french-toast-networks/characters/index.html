Aussie
Junior Network Technician — recently arrived from Australia.

Already works in networking and understands practical concepts, but has uneven foundations and CCNA-specific gaps (IOS confidence, subnetting speed, STP detail, OSPF mechanics, wireless architecture, precise exam terminology). Learns quickly with a clear mental model.

Season arc
Moves from a capable but uneven technician to an engineer who can explain, verify and troubleshoot confidently.
“Yeah nah, that’s definitely a broadcast domain problem.”

Rafael “Rafa” Costa
Brazilian Lead Network Engineer — the mentor.

Packet-path reasoning, troubleshooting, IOS, routing protocols, subnetting, exam interpretation and simple explanations of hard concepts. Relaxed and playful until a real outage — then extremely methodical.

Signature moves
  • Short questions, patient hints, clear explanations.
  • Uses humour, hints and evidence — never gives intentionally misleading advice.
  • Occasionally practises capoeira in the office.
“Do not touch the configuration yet. Tell me what the device believes.”

Étienne Moreau
Technical Co-Founder and Solutions Architect. Camille’s husband.

Routing, switching, architecture, physical infrastructure and customer presentations. Over-designs small jobs unless Camille intervenes.

Recurring moves
  • Draws topologies on café napkins.
  • Defends French football passionately.
  • Teaches Aussie questionable French slang.
  • Is embarrassed when his mother calls him “mon amour”.

Camille Moreau
Co-Founder and Operations Director. Étienne’s wife.

Wireless, security, operational standards, documentation, change control, automation and programmability. Translates customer needs into technical requirements and prevents unnecessary complexity. Concise, precise, often deadpan.

Story function
  • Turns vague customer asks into deliverable requirements.
  • Tests whether a design can actually be built and maintained.
  • Reinforces documentation and security.

Recurring supporting

Appears when the story needs a normal-customer perspective.

Owner · La Crêpe de MamanÉtienne’s mother
Madame Colette Moreau
Owner of La Crêpe de Maman. Recurring supporting character.

Warm, practical, direct, and impossible to ignore. Treats the payment terminal as critical infrastructure. Describes faults in nontechnical language — and thereby tests whether the engineers can explain things to a normal customer.

Recurring behaviour
  • Calls Étienne “mon amour”.
  • Sends food upstairs during incidents.
  • Knows surprising details about the building.
  • Impatient with jargon.

Cast boundaries

The rules that keep the humour supporting the engineering.

Voice per character

Aussie
Curious, grounded, friendly — asks “why does the device think that?”
Rafa
Short questions, patient hints, clear explanations.
Étienne
Energetic, confident, occasionally dramatic.
Camille
Concise, precise, often deadpan.
Madame Colette
Practical, affectionate, impatient with jargon.

Rules that hold across every session

  • Aussie is inexperienced in some areas, not incompetent.
  • Rafa may joke, but never gives intentionally misleading technical explanations.
  • Étienne and Camille’s banter is affectionate, not hostile.
  • French and Brazilian traits should add flavour without becoming caricatures.
  • French phrases should stay short and understandable from context.
  • Technical accuracy takes priority over comedy.
