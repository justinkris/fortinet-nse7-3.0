DEPARTMENT OF FISH
CHARACTERS REGISTER
Version: 1.0

PURPOSE

Use this file to maintain consistent character behaviour, relationships, responsibilities, and development across all episodes.

CORE CAST

SAMWISE
Role:
- Student character
- Senior Fortinet Administrator
- Gradually becomes the Department’s lead network security architect

Starting profile:
- Experienced with day-to-day FortiGate administration
- Comfortable with policies, interfaces, common troubleshooting, and operational changes
- Less experienced with enterprise architecture, multi-site design, central management at scale, and explaining design decisions

Personality:
- Dependable, practical, calm under ordinary pressure
- Prefers understanding why a technology exists before memorising configuration
- Will challenge vague explanations
- Sometimes moves too quickly into configuration before confirming the decision process

Story development:
- Season 1: Learns to think beyond a single appliance
- Season 2: Takes ownership of resilience decisions
- Season 3: Learns to control change at scale
- Season 4: Becomes evidence-driven in security investigations
- Season 5: Designs the national overlay
- Season 6: Operates and communicates as an architect

Do not:
- Portray Samwise as a beginner
- Make direct Lord of the Rings plot parallels
- Overuse quotes, references, or jokes based on the name

SEÑOR GONZALEZ
Role:
- Quirky Senior Network Security Architect
- Samwise’s mentor
- Technical authority who guides through questions rather than immediately supplying answers

Appearance:
- Always wears a sombrero
- The sombrero may occasionally be impractical in doorways, lifts, racks, or meeting rooms
- Treat this as a recurring visual detail, not the entire joke

Personality:
- Patient, perceptive, unconventional
- Uses memorable but technically accurate analogies
- Often asks one precise question that exposes the real design issue
- Enjoys coffee and carries a battered engineering notebook
- Occasionally gives a strange warning that becomes relevant later

Teaching behaviour:
- Does not lecture for pages
- Allows Samwise to reason first
- Gives direct correction when Samwise is stuck
- Praises sound engineering reasoning, not merely correct terminology
- Gradually shifts from mentor to peer

Recurring phrases should be used sparingly:
- “Do not configure the answer before you understand the question.”
- “The packet does not care what the diagram promised.”
- “Show me the decision point.”

JUNIOR
Role:
- Keen junior network engineer
- Learns from Samwise
- Tests whether Samwise can explain concepts clearly

Personality:
- Enthusiastic, diligent, curious
- Takes detailed notes
- Sometimes proposes a plausible but incomplete solution
- Asks natural questions that expose exam distinctions
- Never asks obscure trivia simply to make Samwise look wrong

Function in lessons:
- Asks why a feature exists
- Challenges confusing terms
- Asks what happens during failure
- Requests one useful command or verification method
- Helps convert Samwise’s knowledge into teachable mental models

Development:
- Begins by following procedures
- Gradually learns to predict behaviour
- By Season 6, can independently isolate basic faults

AISHA KHAN

MEL CHEN
Role:
- FortiManager and Automation Specialist
Personality:
- Organised, exacting, quietly funny

DAVE “RACKS” O’CONNOR
Role:
- Data Centre Engineer
Personality:

- Describes topology in terms of cables, power, optics, and failure domains
Story use:

PRIYA NAIR
Role:

Personality:

- Wants technical risk translated into application impact
Story use:

LEE MURPHY
Role:

- Frequently provides the first imperfect but valuable clue
Story use:

DR ELEANOR FINCH
Role:

Personality:
- Decisive, politically aware, not deeply technical

TOMAS REED

CHARACTER RULES

- Keep story openings under approximately 150 words unless the scene itself is the exercise.
- Use no more than three active characters in a normal lesson scene.
- Characters must serve the learning objective.
- Do not allow comedy to undermine technical credibility.
- Maintain continuity from the latest session summary.
- Add new recurring characters to this file only when they are likely to return.
- Minor one-off characters may appear without being added.
