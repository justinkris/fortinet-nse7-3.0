DEPARTMENT OF FISH
AGENCY REGISTER
Version: 1.0

AGENCY IDENTITY

Name:
Department of Fish

Type:
Fictional Australian Government department

Mission:
Protect and manage fisheries, marine ecosystems, licensing, research, compliance, and national aquatic biosecurity.

Tone:
Realistic government operations with light humour. The Department is competent but constrained by budgets, procurement, legacy systems, audit requirements, and rapid growth.

INITIAL ORGANISATION

Staff:
Approximately 100–150 users on one office floor in Tuggeranong.

Primary location:
Tuggeranong, Canberra
Informal name: Tuggers

Secondary data centre:
Gungahlin, Canberra
Informal name: Gunners

Initial relationship:
- The office floor is attached to the Tuggers data centre.
- Gunners is the secondary data centre.
- Tuggers and Gunners are connected by fibre.
- Both data centres contain FortiGate HA clusters.
- FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer are part of the initial management platform.
- The initial network contains a small set of VLANs and relatively simple routing.

INITIAL BUSINESS SERVICES

- Finance and procurement services
- Identity and directory services
- Staff internet and collaboration services

INITIAL TECHNICAL STATE

Strengths:
- Experienced Fortinet operations staff
- Two data-centre locations
- Existing FortiGate platform

Technical debt:
- Some manually maintained configuration
- Inconsistent object naming

- Static or simplistic routing in parts of the network
- Incomplete documentation of service dependencies
- Disaster-recovery assumptions not fully tested

GROWTH ROADMAP

Stage 1 — Canberra foundation
- One office floor
- Tuggers primary data centre
- Gunners secondary data centre
- Basic segmentation and routing

Stage 2 — Resilience
- HA validation
- Dynamic routing between data centres
- Tested service failover
- Clear failure domains

Stage 3 — Australian expansion
- Sydney and Brisbane first
- Melbourne, Perth, and Darwin later
- Centralised policy and provisioning
- Site-to-site VPN and eventually ADVPN

Stage 4 — National maturity
- Standardised branch templates
- Security Fabric visibility
- Stronger security-profile design
- FortiManager-led change control

Stage 5 — Global operations
- International research partnerships and offices
- Cloud-hosted workloads
- Higher traffic volumes
- More complex BGP policy
- Hardware acceleration and performance engineering

FORTIMANAGER STARTING MODEL

Initial recommendation:
- One primary ADOM for the Department unless a story requirement justifies separation
- Separate policy packages when business role or lifecycle materially differs
- Shared objects where intent is genuinely common
- Dynamic mappings and metadata variables for site-specific values
- Controlled installation with preview and revision history

Do not create ADOMs merely because sites are geographically separate.
