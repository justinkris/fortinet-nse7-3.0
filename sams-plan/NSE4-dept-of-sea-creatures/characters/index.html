The Characters

Three core, one recurring specialist, one unseen executive. Samwise is the learner viewpoint. Señor teaches through terrible jokes with sound engineering underneath. Junior exposes exam traps. Dr Eleanor asks the uncomfortable question. Fysh applies deadline pressure from behind a fish icon.

Not a complete beginner. Understands general networking, is still developing Fortinet depth, troubleshooting discipline and architectural judgement. Teaching Junior is how Samwise proves mastery.

Señor Gonzalez
Samwise's peer. Highly knowledgeable. Makes so many jokes people struggle to tell when he's serious.

Rule: he may present the point in a ridiculous way, but the engineering point underneath must be correct.

Common behaviour
  • Creates memorable analogies.
  • Notices missing evidence.
  • Occasionally says the exact exam answer between two terrible puns.
“Was that a TCP FIN, or an actual fin?”

Graduate engineerTrap-exposer
Junior
Keen graduate engineer. Blames the firewall before collecting evidence.

Changes several variables at once, assumes green means fully healthy, and treats one successful stage as proof of end-to-end success. Junior's mistakes expose common exam traps without making him look incompetent.

Growth arc
Learns to establish a baseline, change one thing at a time, and distinguish policy, routing, session and inspection failures.

Recurring specialist

Appears when the evidence is ambiguous or the team is about to change something without proving the cause.

Dr Eleanor
Recurring Principal Security Architect · Incident Response & Threat Specialist

Calm, precise, difficult to impress. She doesn't solve every problem. Instead, she asks the uncomfortable question that reveals the team's missing assumption — then goes back to her notebook.

Appears when
  • The evidence is ambiguous.
  • A short-term fix creates architectural risk.
  • The team needs to separate incident containment from root-cause analysis.

Fysh
Head of the Department of Sea Creatures.

Appears only in Teams. Never turns on the camera. Uses a fish silhouette as the profile icon. Issues broad, deadline-heavy instructions. Has never been observed entering or leaving a building. Nobody agrees on whether Fysh is a surname, title, shared executive account, AI, or an actual fish with excellent delegation skills.

“Please make the network secure, scalable, resilient and cost-neutral before the next tide.”
