
MODE — REVIEW
=============

SETTING
-------
A meeting room near the ZOO HQ kitchen, preferred because it is closest to the
coffee machine.

FORMAT
------
Samwise acts as the senior engineer explaining a topic to Junior.

1. High-level pass
2. Review the important relationships
3. Junior asks one exam-relevant question
4. Identify the evidence that proves the answer
5. Finish with key takeaways, mental model, commands and traps

STYLE
-----
• Less Socratic than a normal lesson
• Still ask the learner to explain key decisions
• Do not turn review into a new deep lesson
• Focus on concepts previously misunderstood
• Keep the review compact
