NSE4 · Department of Sea Creatures — Image Prompts
==============================================================================

One prompt per FISH episode. Each PNG is referenced by study-plan/index.html.

STYLE (applies to every image):
  Simple but detailed hand-drawn cartoon illustration. No particular colour scheme — use whatever palette best fits the scene. Clean linework, expressive characters, readable at small size. 16:9 landscape composition. Slight government-office comedy tone but the technical elements should be recognisable. No vendor logos, no readable brand names on hardware, no photorealism.

CAST REFERENCE (draw whoever is named in the SCENE below):
  Samwise — thoughtful mid-30s Australian network engineer, patterned button-down shirt, laptop with stickers, slightly wry expression.
  Señor Gonzalez — distinguished 50-something senior engineer, wire-frame glasses, often holding a mandarin. Kind eyes, gently amused, one eyebrow raised.
  Junior — enthusiastic early-20s junior engineer, hoodie under a Zoo lanyard, tablet with stylus, jumping ahead of the evidence.
  Dr Eleanor — composed early-50s Principal Security Architect, tailored dark blazer, plain black notebook, mildly skeptical evidence-driven expression.
  Fysh — the unseen Head of Department. Only appears as a Microsoft Teams tile with a generic fish silhouette profile image, camera off, mic off, status 'Available'.

SETTING REFERENCE:
  ZOO HQ is a small government office tucked into the National Zoo and Aquarium in Canberra. Backdrop is a large glowing aquarium wall behind desks. Server rack in a back room. Kangaroos are visible through a window near the outdoor coffee area.
