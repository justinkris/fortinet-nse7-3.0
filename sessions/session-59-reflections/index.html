Concepts Covered 9 concepts
  1. 01
    THE THREE RIBs (pp. 340-341) — the session's spine. The student ASKED FOR THIS EXPLICITLY ("Can you explain the RIB, I am getting confused"), which was the right call and was rewarded. - RIB-in = everything I was told, raw, unprocessed, pre-filter. "Received." - Local RIB = what I chose to believe. Survived the IN filter, next hop resolves, won best-path. - RIB-out = what I am willing to say to my peers. NOT automatically the same as Local RIB. Framing used
    THREE DRAWERS, left to right, with a gate at each boundary.
  2. 02
    TWO GATES ON THE RIB-in → Local RIB BOUNDARY, taught as a pair because the student named only one
    - Gate 1: the IN filter (prefix-list-in, route-map-in) — "do I even want this?" - Gate 2: validity (is the NEXT_HOP resolvable?) + best-path selection. The student derived Gate 2 from the S58 fault scar, unprompted and correctly.
  3. 03
    THE LOCAL RIB → RIB-out GATE AS THE HOME OF ALL POLICY. This was the session's biggest structural payoff and should be REUSED CONSTANTLY IN S60+. Everything taught across S58/S59 lives at this one door
    IBGP split horizon, RR relay rules, next-hop-self, prefix-list-out. All of them answer ONE question: "of the things I believe, what am I willing to say, and to whom?" Señor's frame: BELIEVING AND TELLING ARE DIFFERENT DRAWERS.
  4. 04
    IBGP SPLIT HORIZON AS THE CAUSE OF FULL MESH. Retrieved cold (after one prompt) in exact wording
    "a prefix learned from an IBGP peer is never advertised to ANY OTHER IBGP PEER." The student had to be walked to it once but produced it verbatim. Now OWNED.
  5. 05
    ROUTE REFLECTORS AS A DELIBERATE RELAXATION OF SPLIT HORIZON — framed not as a new mechanism but as the removal of a safety rule
    "The RR is breaking the split-horizon rule on purpose." An RR + its clients = a CLUSTER. Clients peer ONLY with the RR. The RR peers with other RRs and border routers. Mesh collapses from n(n-1)/2 to n.
  6. 06
    THE CLIENT IS MUTE AND DOESN'T KNOW IT'S A CLIENT. Critical exam point, delivered twice. `set route-reflector-client enable` is configured ONLY ON THE RR, under the neighbour block for each client. Study guide wording
    "The RR defines which neighbors are the clients." The client's config is an ORDINARY IBGP neighbour statement. Nothing on it says "client." Directional-trap teaching technique used: read the SHAPE of the command — everything inside `config neighbor / edit <ip>` describes THE PEER AT THAT ADDRESS. Compared explicitly to `set remote-as` and `set next-hop-self` to generalise the rule.
  7. 07
    REPLACEMENT LOOP PREVENTION. Taught by ASKING BEFORE TELLING per the S58 handoff instruction, and the student PREDICTED IT (see performance). - ORIGINATOR_ID — router-ID of the client that originally injected the route. If a router sees its own router-ID here, it drops the route SILENTLY. Router-scope. - CLUSTER_LIST — cluster-ID prepended by each reflecting RR. If an RR sees its own cluster-ID, it drops the route. Cluster-scope. THIS IS THE INTER-CLUSTER / MULTI-RR GUARD. Presented as three NESTED RINGS, all asking the same question at different scales
    Between ASes → AS_PATH → "is my AS in it?" Between clusters→ CLUSTER_LIST → "is my cluster-ID in it?" Within a cluster→ ORIGINATOR_ID→ "is my router-ID it?" "Same question at every scale: did this come from me?"
  8. 08
    THE THREE RR RELAY CASES. Student over-tightened here twice; needed correcting. - From a CLIENT → reflect to EVERYONE (other clients, non-client IBGP peers, EBGP peers). Justification the student must hold
    the client can't tell anyone itself, so if the RR doesn't relay, NOBODY hears it. - From a NON-CLIENT IBGP peer → reflect TO CLIENTS ONLY. Not to other non-clients (those are ordinary IBGP peers; original split horizon still binds). - From an EBGP peer → advertise to everyone. Split horizon never applied. COMPRESSED RULE GIVEN TO THE STUDENT (reuse this exact line): "Downward (to clients): always. Sideways (non-client to non-client): never." Señor's version: "The reflector speaks freely to its own. To strangers, it only repeats what its own told it."
  9. 09
    THE OPERATIONAL PAYOFF, stated in Penny's language
    adding Adelaide = one neighbour block on HQ with route-reflector-client enable, plus one ordinary neighbour block on Adelaide. TWO ROUTERS TOUCHED, NOT NINE. Eleven hours becomes twenty minutes. ==================================================================
