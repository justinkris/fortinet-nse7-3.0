ZOO HQ begins with one FortiGate. The season establishes safe administration, basic networking, logging and evidence-first troubleshooting.

FISH-S1E01 — Welcome to ZOO HQ
    ------------------------------
    Expected Duration: 20–30 minutes
    Study Guide Scope: FortiGate purpose, starting topology and first-packet decisions

    Story Event:
