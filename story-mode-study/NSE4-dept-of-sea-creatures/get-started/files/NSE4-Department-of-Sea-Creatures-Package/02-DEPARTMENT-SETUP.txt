
DEPARTMENT SETUP
================

SETTING
-------
The Department of Sea Creatures is an Australian Government agency headquartered
inside the National Zoo and Aquarium in Canberra. The headquarters is called ZOO HQ.

The NSE4 story follows the Division of Fish as it builds and secures its first
FortiGate environment.

CORE CHARACTERS
---------------
SAMWISE
Samwise is the learner viewpoint and Junior's mentor. He begins as a capable
network engineer who understands general networking but is still developing
Fortinet knowledge, troubleshooting discipline and architectural judgement.

SEÑOR GONZALEZ
Señor is Samwise's peer. He is highly knowledgeable but makes so many jokes that
people sometimes struggle to tell when he is serious. The engineering point under
the joke should remain correct.

JUNIOR
Junior is a keen graduate engineer who often blames the firewall before collecting
evidence, changes several variables at once, assumes green means fully healthy and
treats one successful stage as proof of end-to-end success.

DR ELEANOR
Dr Eleanor is a recurring Principal Security Architect and Incident Response and
Threat Specialist. She appears when evidence is ambiguous or the team is about to
make a broad change without proving the cause.

FYSH
Fysh runs the Department. No one has met Fysh in person. Fysh appears only in
Teams meetings, never turns on the camera and uses a generic fish silhouette as the
profile icon. Never reveal whether Fysh is a surname, title, shared account, AI or
an actual fish.

TONE
----
Use grounded technical workplace scenarios, light government-office comedy,
occasional stronger sitcom moments and fish or sea-creature puns. The story creates
the operational requirement; it does not replace the technical explanation.
