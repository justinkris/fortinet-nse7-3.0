DEPARTMENT OF FISH
FORTINET NSE 7 SECURE NETWORKING ARCHITECT
36-EPISODE STORY STUDY PLAN

FORMAT

- Target session length: 20–30 minutes
- One or two connected mental models per episode
- Persistent Department topology
- Optional mini-labs only
- Gradual evidence reveal during troubleshooting
- Progressive Socratic hints
- 5–10 exam questions depending on time and topic breadth

SEASON 1 — THE FIRST CURRENT
The Department operates from Telstra Tower, with data centres in Tuggers and Gunners, and already has a basic SD-WAN deployment.

EP-01 — The Two Roads Out of Tuggers
Topics: SD-WAN fundamentals, members, zones, rules, health checks, underlay versus overlay.
Story: users at Telstra Tower experience inconsistent performance across the two WAN paths terminating through Tuggers.
Mental models: member = road; health check = measurement; rule = new-session decision.
Topology: two active WAN members at Tuggers.

EP-02 — Up Is Not Good
Topics: member health, performance SLA, latency, jitter, packet loss, alive versus compliant.
Story: the main link remains reachable but becomes unusable for voice and licensing.
Mental models: up is a fact; good is a measurement; probe is thermometer; SLA is fever line.

EP-03 — The First Packet Chooses
Topics: new sessions, SD-WAN rule lookup, existing sessions, session table, reevaluation.
Story: a route changes, but existing users stay on the old path.
Mental model: first packet visits the front desk; later packets use the access card.

EP-04 — The Implicit Rule
Topics: explicit rules, implicit rule, FIB influence, ordering.
Story: unmatched traffic takes a path nobody expected.
Mental model: explicit intent first; implicit fallback still uses routing knowledge.

EP-05 — Who Wins?
Topics: manual, best quality, lowest cost, load balance, priority advantage.
Story: Señor Gonzalez asks Samwise to justify four different strategies for four applications.
Mental models:
- Manual: which member is ordered first and alive?
- Best quality: who is winning now?
- Lowest cost: who passes the bar, then preference?
- Load balance: how should new sessions be spread?

EP-06 — Direct to the Internet
Topics: DIA topologies, local breakout, central breakout, best practices.
Story: Sydney wants SaaS traffic to exit locally.
Season ending: architecture review.

SEASON 2 — CONTROL BEFORE SCALE
The Department must deploy branches without hand-building each one.

EP-07 — The Branch in a Box
Topics: ZTP, device deployment, device blueprints, CSV import.
Story: thirty future branch appliances are ordered.

EP-08 — FortiManager for SD-WAN
Topics: SD-WAN Manager, central versus per-device management, ADOM scope.
Story: Mel asks what should be shared and what must remain local.

EP-09 — One Template, Many Values
Topics: metadata variables, defaults, per-device mappings, interfaces, gateways, probe targets.
Story: Sydney and Brisbane share the same intent but not the same local values.

EP-10 — Template Groups
Topics: templates, groups, installation targets, optional members.
Story: some branches have MPLS and LTE; others have only dual internet.

EP-11 — The Overlay Wizard
Topics: orchestration prerequisites, overlay pools, loopbacks, AS numbers, topology planning.
Story: the Department converts a basic branch design into a managed overlay.
Topology: Tuggers becomes the initial hub.

EP-12 — The Second Hub
Topics: single-to-dual-hub conversion, primary/secondary roles, branch groups.
Story: Gunners becomes the secondary SD-WAN hub.
Season ending: orchestration incident.

SEASON 3 — ROUTES THAT EXPLAIN THEMSELVES
Policy and convergence now matter more than simple connectivity.

EP-13 — OSPF Across the Estate
Topics: OSPF, access lists, prefix lists, route maps, redistribution.
Story: legacy OSPF domains must exchange selected routes with the new overlay.

EP-14 — OSPF Over IPsec
Topics: OSPF over tunnels, ECMP, failure behaviour.
Story: equal-cost tunnel paths exist, but Samwise must predict forwarding and recovery.

EP-15 — BGP Belief
Topics: received, accepted, selected, installed.
Story: Brisbane receives a route that never appears in the routing table.
Mental model: received is not accepted; accepted is not selected; selected is not always installed.

EP-16 — BGP Policy Tools
Topics: access lists, prefix lists, route maps, redistribution, ECMP.
Story: Tuggers should be preferred for some prefixes and Gunners for others.

EP-17 — Loopbacks and Neighbour Groups
Topics: loopback-sourced BGP, neighbor-group, scalable peer configuration.
Story: branch growth makes repeated peer configuration operational debt.

EP-18 — Fast Enough to Matter
Topics: BFD, graceful restart, route reflectors, convergence.
Story: failover works, but not fast enough for a critical application.
Season ending: integrated routing incident.

SEASON 4 — THE SESSION REMEMBERS
Routing, SD-WAN, and session state collide.

EP-19 — Policy Routes Before SD-WAN
Topics: lookup order, policy routes, SD-WAN rules, FIB.
Story: a policy route silently overrides an SD-WAN rule.
Mental model: policy route > SD-WAN rule > FIB.

EP-20 — Member Routes and Probe Routes
Topics: member static routes, zone routes, probe routes, health-check path.
Story: monitoring succeeds while user traffic fails.

EP-21 — Dirty Sessions
Topics: protocol states, session flags, dirty sessions, reevaluation triggers.
Story: a route change marks sessions dirty, but not all sessions move immediately.

EP-22 — SNAT Route Change
Topics: translated sessions, snat-route-change, retain versus kill and rebuild.
Story: internet failover affects SNAT sessions differently from expectations.

EP-23 — Local-Out Traffic
Topics: FortiGate-generated traffic, management flows, probe flows, local-out steering.
Story: the FortiGate itself uses a different path from clients.

EP-24 — Application Learning
Topics: application steering, learning phases, ISDB destinations, classification timing.
Story: a new application does not steer immediately.
Season ending: rules-and-sessions architecture review.

SEASON 5 — TRUST AND CONTROL
The Department automates security without losing visibility.

EP-25 — The Fabric Connects
Topics: Fabric connectors versus external connectors, SAML SSO.
Story: a central access requirement exposes connector confusion.

EP-26 — The Stitch Fires
Topics: automation stitches, triggers, actions, quarantine, IoC response.
Story: a compromised endpoint must be contained automatically.

EP-27 — FortiNAC and Dynamic Addressing
Topics: FortiNAC, dynamic firewall addresses, FortiNDR integration.
Story: endpoint posture must dynamically change policy membership.

EP-28 — Automation Under Pressure
Topics: backups, CLI scripts, high-CPU automation.
Story: a high-CPU incident forces Samwise to decide what can safely be automated.

EP-29 — FGCP Beyond the Basics
Topics: active-active, virtual clustering, virtual MAC, Ethernet types, sync optimisation, VDOM partitioning.
Story: high traffic volume forces a more advanced HA design.

EP-30 — FGSP and Asymmetry
Topics: standalone sync, coverage and limits, encrypted sync over IPsec, asymmetric traffic, FGCP versus FGSP versus VRRP.
Story: a cloud inspection design breaks ordinary clustering assumptions.
Season ending: asymmetric traffic incident.

SEASON 6 — ADVANCED IPSEC
Scale exposes the parts basic tunnel guides leave out.

EP-31 — IKEv2 Under Pressure
Topics: topology design, DPD modes, NAT impact, interfaces without IP addresses.
Story: a branch behind NAT behaves differently from a directly addressed site.

EP-32 — Packets Too Large
Topics: MTU, MSS, fragmentation, performance optimisation.
Story: the tunnel is up and routing is correct, but large transfers fail.

EP-33 — Redundant Tunnels
Topics: IPsec aggregate, redundancy, load balancing, overlapping remote routes.
Story: the Department needs redundant encrypted paths without ambiguous routing.

EP-34 — Offload, FEC, and the NPU Flag
Topics: IPsec hardware offload, FEC, session NPU-Flag, troubleshooting accelerated traffic.
Story: performance is excellent but packet evidence is incomplete.

EP-35 — Multihub and Multiregion
Topics: dual-hub options, large topology design, BGP self-healing, multiregion, MSSP use cases, VRF-aware overlays.
Story: the Department expands globally.
Topology: Australian region plus first international region.

EP-36 — ADVPN 1.0 to 2.0
Topics: shortcut negotiation, FortiManager deployment, IBGP/EBGP, placeholders, timeout, failback, dependent shortcuts, BGP on loopback, no-route-reflection designs, dynamic BGP, ADVPN 1.0 versus 2.0.
Story: Samwise redesigns the national overlay for direct spoke-to-spoke paths without losing control.
Final incident: dual-hub, multiregion ADVPN failure.
Final outcome: Samwise presents the architecture as the Department’s secure networking architect.

SECURITY PROFILE REINFORCEMENT

Security profiles are lower-weight than routing and advanced IPsec but remain examinable.

Insert focused reinforcement when the knowledge register shows weakness in:
- certificate inspection versus full inspection
- protecting clients versus protecting SSL servers
- SNI checks
- certificate errors
- false positives
- HTTP/HTTPS code injection
- CVE-based IPS patterns
- web filter, application control, IPS, and ISDB combinations
- performance impact

EXAM QUESTION RULE

Use 5–10 questions according to:
- session length
- topic breadth
- mastery
- remaining time

Ask one at a time.
Do not reveal the answer before Samwise responds.
