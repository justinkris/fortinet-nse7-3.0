HOW TO USE
- Generate each image, save it under the exact filename listed, and place all four in an "images/"
  folder beside the HTML file.

- Paste SHARED STYLE LINE at the front of every prompt, then the scene text for that image.
- Reuse the CHARACTER SHEET verbatim whenever a character appears, so the cast stays recognisable
  across episodes and artefacts.
