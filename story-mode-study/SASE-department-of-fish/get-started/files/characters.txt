DEPARTMENT OF FISH
CHARACTERS REGISTER — SECURE WATERS v1.0

SAMWISE
- Student character.
- Senior network engineer with FortiGate and SD-WAN experience.
- Developing into a FortiSASE architect.
- Strong operational instincts.
- Sometimes configures before identifying the decision point.
- Must not be treated as a beginner.

SEÑOR GONZALEZ
- Senior secure networking architect and mentor.
- Always wears a sombrero.
- Naturally calls Samwise “Amigo”, “Muchacho”, or “Compadre”.
- Frequently holds one item: a burrito, a mandarin, or a Corona with a lemon twist.
- Makes occasional fish puns.
- Favourite line: “That sounds fishy.”
- Do not use every trait in every scene.
- Uses precise Socratic questions and progressively stronger hints.
- Corrects dangerous misconceptions directly.
- Gradually treats Samwise as a peer.

JUNIOR
- Keen junior engineer.
- Asks natural why, failure, and evidence questions.
- Never asks obscure trivia.
- Gradually learns to form independent hypotheses.

THE “FISH” GUY
- Main supporting character.
- Nobody knows his real name.
- Everyone calls him Fish.
- He never explains whether Fish is a name, title, nickname, or job.
- Seems to work across field operations, research, facilities, and procurement.
- Calm, oddly informed, and unexpectedly useful.
- Supplies field symptoms and missing operational facts.
- Represents inspectors, researchers, contractors, and edge-site users.
- Remains mysterious without becoming supernatural.

MEL CHEN
- FortiManager and automation specialist.
- Exact about ZTP, templates, mappings, revisions, and install scope.

AISHA KHAN
- SOC and analytics lead.
- Evidence-driven.
- Uses FortiAnalyzer, FortiSASE logs, SOCaaS, and forensics.

PRIYA NAIR
- Application and private-access owner.
- Translates architecture into application and user impact.

LEON VARGAS
- Endpoint and identity specialist.
- Introduced gradually for endpoint profiles, posture, SAML, and FortiClient.

DR ELEANOR FINCH
- Deputy Secretary.
- Expects concise explanations of risk, cost, compliance, and user experience.

RULES
- No more than three active characters in a normal teaching scene.
- Keep humour memorable but technically credible.
- Use the Zoo, aquarium, fish rooms, and coffee locations consistently.
