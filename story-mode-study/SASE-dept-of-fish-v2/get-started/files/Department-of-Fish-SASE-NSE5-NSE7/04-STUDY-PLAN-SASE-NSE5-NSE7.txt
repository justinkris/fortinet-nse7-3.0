SASE-S1E01 — The Perimeter Left the Aquarium
--------------------------------------------
Expected Duration:
    20–30 minutes

Study Guide / Blueprint Scope:
    Traditional architecture challenges; SASE; SSE; SD-WAN relationship

Story Event:
    MYBAD opens with remote staff, SaaS and citizen reports. Backhauling every flow through ZOO HQ damages voice quality.

Learning Objectives:
    • Explain the problems SASE addresses
    • Define SASE as SD-WAN plus cloud-delivered SSE
    • Identify security and user-experience goals

Core Mental Model:
    SASE combines networking and cloud-delivered security so controls follow users and applications.

Socratic Direction:
    Begin with a prediction from the story.
    Ask one question at a time.
    Require the learner to identify the decision point and the evidence that
    would prove or disprove the answer.
    Change one condition before closing.

Exam Relevance:
    Test behaviour, use-case selection, evidence interpretation and closely
    related mechanisms rather than isolated definition recall.

    FortiSASE tenant authorised.
