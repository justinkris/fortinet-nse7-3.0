OPTIONAL LAB INDEX — SASE PATH
================================

LAB RULES
---------
Labs are optional and must not block story progress.

Before each lab include:

Think First
    One prediction question.

Success Criteria
    What must be observable when the lab works.

Evidence
    The GUI, log, route, session, tunnel, endpoint or packet evidence required.

Recovery
    How to return to the known-good state.
