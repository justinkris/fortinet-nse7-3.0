PUBLIC CONTACT OPERATION
------------------------
Name:
    MYBAD

Expanded name:
    Marine Yield, Biosecurity and Aquatic Dispatch

Purpose:
    A public contact and incident intake service handling calls, reports,
    photographs and case information from across Australia.

Why it matters to the story:
    MYBAD creates remote workers, SaaS use, voice quality requirements,
    citizen-data protection and private-application access requirements.

CORE LOCATIONS
--------------

STARTING TECHNICAL ENVIRONMENT
------------------------------
The story begins with:

• One FortiSASE tenant
• Managed FortiClient users
• Temporary agentless users
• Department identity services
• One private application at ZOO HQ
• Internet and SaaS access
• Basic logs and dashboards

The environment later grows to include:

• MYBAD SD-WAN edge
• Tuggers and Gunners hubs
• FortiManager
• FortiAnalyzer
• Dual-hub SPA
• FortiSASE as an SD-WAN spoke
• Thin-edge and FortiAP sites
• Multiregion design
• Advanced endpoint posture
• Central analytics and troubleshooting
