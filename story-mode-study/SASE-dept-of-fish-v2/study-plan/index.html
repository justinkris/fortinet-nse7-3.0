NSE 5 Core Administrator · NSE 7 Architect

Story-Based Study Plan

Six seasons, 41 Socratic episodes. Samwise opens by rescuing the MYBAD call centre from backhauled voice, walks the whole NSE 5 core-admin path, and steps up to NSE 7 architect — ending on the Missing Fish Incident where every layer of the design has to hold under simultaneous failure.
