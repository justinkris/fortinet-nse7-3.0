DEPARTMENT OF FISH — CHARACTERS
===============================

SAMWISE
-------
Role:
    Network and security engineer; the learner.

Development:
    Begins as the engineer being coached.
    Gradually becomes the senior who explains concepts to Junior.
    By NSE7, Samwise must defend architectural choices and troubleshooting logic.

Teaching use:
    Samwise should be asked to predict, explain and justify.
    Do not make Samwise passive in long lectures.

JUNIOR
------
Role:
    Keen graduate engineer and Samwise's mentee.

Personality:
    Intelligent, energetic and helpful.
    Often jumps to the first plausible mechanism.
    Makes realistic exam-style mistakes rather than foolish mistakes.

Teaching use:
    Junior voices common misconceptions.
    At the end of many sessions, Junior asks one exam-relevant question.
    Samwise should increasingly answer Junior in plain language.

SEÑOR GONZALEZ
--------------
Role:
    Samwise's experienced peer.

Personality:
    Very knowledgeable.
    Frequently jokes.
    Sometimes difficult to take seriously.
    Uses terms such as Amigo, Muchacho and Compadre.
    Occasionally says, “That sounds fishy.”
    May appear with a burrito, mandarin or a Corona with a lemon twist.

Teaching use:
    Provides memorable mental models.
    Must always translate the joke into accurate technical language.

DR ELEANOR
----------
Role:
    Security architect and difficult-incident specialist.

Personality:
    Calm, precise and evidence-driven.
    Challenges vague explanations.
    Frequently asks what observation would disprove the current theory.

Teaching use:
    Appears for architecture, zero trust, data protection, posture,
    multi-fault incidents and advanced troubleshooting.

FYSH
----
Role:
    Department head.

Presentation:
    No one has seen Fysh in person.
    Fysh appears only in Teams meetings.
    Camera is always off.
    Teams icon is a silhouette of a fish.

Teaching use:
    Introduces constraints, deadlines, audits and executive questions.
    Often asks for a one-sentence explanation or design justification.

SUPPORTING CHARACTERS
---------------------
Baz
    Operations analyst who trusts green dashboards too quickly.

Penny
    Service-desk lead who brings high-quality user symptoms.

Riley
    Platform engineer who provides deployment and automation context.

The Fish Guy
    Unnamed supporting character.
    Appears briefly and is never fully explained.

CHARACTER GUARDRAILS
--------------------
• Humour supports memory, not distraction.
• No character should deliberately provide technically false advice without the
  session explicitly correcting it.
• Junior must remain capable.
• Dr Eleanor should not solve every problem immediately.
• Samwise's growth must be visible across the seasons.
