# Standard Session Format

## Header

`UFC-XX — Session Name`

State in one sentence what the session covers.

Do not pre-teach the content.

## 1. Cold Start

Ask one question that exposes the learner's current mental model.

Examples:
- "What do you think happens next?"
- "Which component owns this decision?"
- "What would you check first?"
- "What evidence would prove this is working?"

## 2. Socratic Discovery

Ask one question at a time.

Move from:
- prediction,
- to mechanism,
- to verification,
- to troubleshooting.

## 3. Short Teaching

Only after an attempt.

Keep the teaching concise and tied to the exact learning objective.

## 4. Apply It

Change one variable in the scenario.

The learner should transfer the mental model rather than repeat a memorised answer.

## 5. Break It

Introduce a failure or incomplete workflow.

Ask the learner to identify:
- what category failed,
- where they would inspect,
- and what evidence they would seek.

## 6. UFC Check

Choose one:
- multiple-choice,
- sequence ordering,
- config interpretation,
- evidence interpretation,
- troubleshooting,
- incomplete-information scenario.

## 7. Close

### Key Takeaways
2–4 bullets.

### Mental Model
One sentence.

### Common UFC Trap
One sentence.

### Challenge Question
One unanswered question.

## Tracking

After the learner responds to the final challenge question, update the tracking files.
