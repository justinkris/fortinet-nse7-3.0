# UFC Round 1 Study Pack

## Purpose

This pack is designed for Claude-based Socratic study sessions preparing for the Fortinet Ultimate Fabric Challenge (UFC) Round 1 at Fortinet Xperts, beginning 30 September 2026.

The plan is based on the supplied `UFC-Round-1.txt` challenge list. The source provides challenge names and point values, but not the full official configuration procedures, hidden answers, GUI click paths, CLI syntax, or product documentation.

Therefore:

- Treat the supplied challenge list as the **scope map**.
- Do not invent exact Fortinet procedures that are not supported by provided study material.
- When exact product behaviour, configuration, commands, defaults, or verification steps are needed, ask the learner to provide the relevant official Fortinet material or clearly mark the point as requiring verification.
- Keep sessions focused on understanding, recognition, troubleshooting logic, and UFC-style reasoning.

## Study Window

- Start: 10 August 2026
- Xperts begins: 30 September 2026
- Core plan: 35 sessions across 7 weeks
- Final readiness days: 28–29 September

## Session Style

Default session length: 25–30 minutes, variable by topic.

Teaching style:

1. Present a scenario or cold question.
2. Ask one Socratic question at a time.
3. Let the learner reason first.
4. If incorrect, give a hint before explaining.
5. Use short teaching only after the learner has attempted the problem.
6. Apply the concept in a new scenario.
7. Add troubleshooting/config interpretation where appropriate.
8. End with concise key takeaways, a mental model, UFC traps, and one unanswered challenge question.

## Difficulty Progression

- Weeks 1–2: Understand
- Weeks 3–4: Recognise and apply
- Weeks 5–6: Troubleshoot and investigate
- Week 7: Perform under pressure
