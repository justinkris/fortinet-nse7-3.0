FIGHT CAMP — SETTING BIBLE
FABRIC CHALLENGE · UFC ROUND 1
FORTINET ULTIMATE FABRIC CHAMPIONSHIP · XPERTS · 30 SEPTEMBER 2026


THE GYM

Halloran's Boxing Gym and Martial Arts sits on the edge of town in an old red-brick fire station. The engine bay doors were bricked in years ago; a single steel side door with a hand-painted sign is the only way in. Inside it smells like sweat, leather, coffee, and the faint diesel ghost the building never quite lost.

Downstairs is the fight floor. Heavy bags hang in a row along the west wall. A regulation octagon cage takes up the middle of the room, chain link scuffed and repaired in three places. A weight room is boxed off in the back corner, mostly free weights, one bench, one squat rack. A single rusted fire pole runs from the upstairs office down through a hole in the corner of the ceiling — Coach won't let anyone use it, but he won't cover the hole either.


COACH'S OFFICE

Upstairs, above the fight floor, is Coach's office. One long room. West wall: a floor-to-ceiling shelf of VHS tapes and DVDs, every fight Coach has ever coached or watched, catalogued by year. East wall: a whiteboard that never gets erased. It stays covered in Fabric diagrams — arrows between FortiGates, FortiAnalyzer, FortiManager, FortiDLP, FortiAI — because, as Coach says, "a network is just a fight plan you draw before the bell."

A small round table with four folding chairs sits in the middle. This is where the strategy sessions happen. A single window looks down onto the fight floor so Coach can watch a round without leaving his coffee.


THE COFFEE MACHINE

There is exactly one coffee machine, tucked in the corner of the office. It is at least twenty years old, missing a button, and the drip tray leaks. Only Doc trusts it. Everyone else brings their own. Doc claims it makes the best coffee in town because it has never been cleaned.


THE WHITEBOARD

The whiteboard is the beating heart of the office. It gets redrawn every week. Week 1 is a scrawl of arrows labelled information → share → consume → enforce. Later weeks add zones, alert handlers, correlation windows, VIPs, IEC-104 flows, and by Week 7, an entire OT segmentation plan with the catch-all rule crossed out in red.

Nobody is allowed to erase Coach's arrows. If he draws it, it stays.


WEATHER AND OUTSIDE

It rains a lot. The parking lot is patched blacktop with weeds. There is one bench outside the side door where Rocco sits between rounds. Across the street: a laundromat, a Vietnamese place, a bus stop.


WEEK RHYTHM

Camp is seven weeks long. Each week has a fight-training theme that maps to the technical scope for that week. Five sessions per week, one session per day, Monday through Friday. Saturdays are film-study with Doc. Sundays are rest.

Week 1 — Learn to Read the Opponent (Security Fabric intel chain)
Week 2 — Sharpen the Tools (FortiOS 8.0 security workflows)
Week 3 — Build the Defence (Access hardening, Reverse SSL VIP, FortiDLP intro)
Week 4 — Policy Footwork (FortiDLP policy reasoning)
Week 5 — Cage Safety (OT foundations + SOC correlation)
Week 6 — Read the Tape (Follow the attack chain)
Week 7 — Fight Week (OT Segmentation + full simulation)


FIGHT NIGHT

30 September 2026. Xperts. Rocco's first fight at this level. He steps into the octagon at the Ultimate Fabric Championship Round 1 and has to demonstrate everything he learned in camp. The audience is Fortinet judges. The clock is real. The scoring is real.

The whiteboard in Coach's office will still have the Week 7 diagram on it when Rocco leaves for the fight. Coach will not erase it. He never does.
