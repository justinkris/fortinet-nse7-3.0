CAST BIBLE — FIGHT CAMP
FABRIC CHALLENGE · UFC ROUND 1


ROCCO "ROCC" DELVECCHIO — THE FIGHTER

Age: 28. Height: 5'10". Rookie underdog. Grew up in a rust-belt town above his uncle's pizza joint, learned to fight in a converted garage gym before Coach took him in. First shot at Xperts. Knows it.

Style: Technical, hungry, but reckless. Throws before he reads. Chases finishes when he should be checking evidence. Wants to prove himself and sometimes cuts corners doing it.

Voice: Direct. Short. Impatient when he thinks he already knows the answer. Uses "yeah" a lot. Never quite says "I don't know" — says "walk me through it again."

Growth arc: Learning to slow down. Learning that a match won on evidence is worth more than a match won on speed. Learning that the coach is right.


COACH MARCUS "MAC" HALLORAN — THE HEAD COACH

Age: 58. Ex-champion. Won UFC Round 1 himself back in his day, then took a bad cut over his right eye that never healed properly. Forced to retire. Bought the old fire station with the last of his fight money and turned it into Halloran's Gym.

Style: Calm. Patient. Never raises his voice. Never repeats himself either.

Voice: Metaphor-driven. Maps fight logic to network logic. Some go-to lines:
- "Slow it down, Rocc."
- "A network is just a fight plan you draw before the bell."
- "They don't tap out to information — they tap out to enforcement."
- "Configured is not working."
- "Draw it on the board before you throw it in the cage."

Never says a student is wrong. Says "show me the arrow." Points at the whiteboard a lot.

Backstory beat: He knows the traps in Round 1 because he fell into every one of them at Xperts 2001.


BIG MICK O'SULLIVAN — SPARRING PARTNER

Age: 42. Heavyweight veteran, six inches taller than Rocco and forty pounds heavier. Ex-military — did a decade in the infantry before he came home and started fighting. Now he spars for Coach and works security at a pub three nights a week.

Style: Devil's advocate. Plays every opponent Rocco might face. Loves segmentation, defence, and slow methodical pressure. Will not let Rocco advance a position until Rocco can defend it.

Voice: Short sentences. Military cadence. Calls Rocco "kid" — never "Rocc". Some lines:
- "Kid. Defend that."
- "You gave up the fence."
- "Fallback rule ate your work."
- "Zones. Then relationships. In that order."

Never sarcastic. Just direct. If Rocco does something stupid, Mick lets Rocco figure out why.


DOC SILVER — CORNERWOMAN, CUTMAN, FILM ANALYST

Age: 46. The sharpest person in the gym. Started as a physio, moved into corner work, now runs film study and between-round strategy for every fighter Coach trains. Reads logs the way she reads a fighter's footwork — for patterns other people miss.

Style: Quiet. Precise. Speaks only when she has evidence.

Voice: Evidence-first. Never says "I think." Says "the tape says." Some lines:
- "The tape says he shifts his weight on the third breath."
- "Show me the log."
- "First observed alert is not the first compromise."
- "One event is a clue. Ordered events are a story."
- "Configured is intention. Verified is truth."

Runs the Saturday film-study sessions. Keeps a spiral notebook full of frame-by-frame notes. Drinks the office coffee. Nobody else does.

Backstory beat: She was the corner for Coach's championship fight. She was also the corner the night he got cut. She has never forgiven herself for missing the setup.


CAST RULES

- These four characters are the only cast. Do not invent new named humans.
- Non-speaking extras (other fighters, the guy at the front desk, a judge at Xperts) can be mentioned in passing but should not have names or lines.
- Do not overuse catchphrases. One character-line per scene is usually enough.
- Coach uses metaphors sparingly. Let one metaphor land per session, not five.
- Mick's short sentences are load-bearing. Don't soften them.
- Doc almost never speaks first. She responds to something Rocco or Coach says by producing evidence.
