Halloran’s Boxing & Martial Arts

Bought with the last of Coach’s fight money. Nobody has painted over the diesel ghost.

The building smells like sweat, leather, coffee, and the faint diesel ghost the fire crew left behind. Downstairs is the fight floor. Upstairs is the office. Between them — a single rusted fire pole running through a hole in the corner of the ceiling. Coach won’t let anyone use it, but he won’t cover the hole either.

Why here: the rent was inexplicably cheap, the ceilings were tall enough to hang bags, and the old engine bay was already the right shape for a cage.

Coach’s Office · upstairs, above the fight floor

A single window looks down onto the cage so Coach can watch a round without leaving his coffee.

The west wall is a floor-to-ceiling shelf of VHS tapes and DVDs — every fight Coach has ever coached or watched, catalogued by year. The east wall is a whiteboard that never gets erased. It stays covered in Fabric diagrams — arrows between FortiGates, FortiAnalyzer, FortiManager, FortiDLP, FortiAI — because, as Coach says, a network is just a fight plan you draw before the bell.

The Whiteboard · east wall

Redrawn every week. Never erased.

Week 1 is a scrawl of arrows labelled information → share → consume → enforce. Later weeks add zones, alert handlers, correlation windows, VIPs, IEC-104 flows, and by Week 7, an entire OT segmentation plan with the catch-all rule crossed out in red.

Nobody is allowed to erase Coach’s arrows. If he draws it, it stays.

The coffee machine: at least twenty years old, missing a button, drip tray leaks. Only Doc trusts it. She claims it makes the best coffee in town because it has never been cleaned.
04 · Camp Rhythm

Seven weeks. Thirty-five sessions.

Each week has a fight-training theme that maps to the technical scope for that week. Five sessions per week, Monday through Friday. Saturdays are film study with Doc. Sundays are rest.

Week 1Read the Opponent

Security Fabric intel chain. Rocco learns to spot components before he throws hands: authorise, share, consume, enforce.

information → share → consume → enforce
Week 2Sharpen the Tools

FortiOS 8.0 security workflows. Hybrid key exchange, SSL enforcement, sanctioned versus unsanctioned classification. Tools are checked before every round.

mechanism ↔ verification
Week 3Build the Defence

Access hardening, Reverse SSL VIP, FortiDLP introduction. Rocco stops chasing finishes and starts closing his own gaps first.

management ↔ published services ↔ endpoint
Week 4Policy Footwork

FortiDLP policy reasoning — AI policy, actions, file download and origin. Coach makes him place every foot deliberately.

match → action → verify
Week 5Cage Safety

OT foundations and SOC correlation. Mick joins for the OT drills. Doc starts running longer film sessions on Saturdays.

event ↔ incident ↔ correlation
Week 6Read the Tape

Follow the attack chain end-to-end — initial compromise, lateral movement, MSSQL as a movement channel, xp_cmdshell, pivot hosts, RTU activity. Rocco does more listening than throwing.

patient zero → lateral → pivot → target
Week 7Fight Week

OT segmentation, catch-all disabled, granular remote access, protocol inspection, and one full-round simulation. Whiteboard fills to the edges. Coach doesn’t erase anything.

zones → explicit relationships → simulation

Rocco steps into the octagon at the Ultimate Fabric Championship Round 1. Fortinet judges. Real clock. Real scoring.

The Ultimate Fabric Championship · Round 1

First fight at this level for Rocco. Coach has been here before.

Rocco has to demonstrate everything he learned in camp — not the parts he liked, all of it. Coach travels with him. Mick doesn’t. Doc runs the corner.

The whiteboard in Coach’s office will still have the Week 7 diagram on it when Rocco leaves for the fight. Coach will not erase it. He never does.

Coach’s line as Rocco leaves for Xperts: “Slow it down, Rocc. Draw it before you throw it.”
