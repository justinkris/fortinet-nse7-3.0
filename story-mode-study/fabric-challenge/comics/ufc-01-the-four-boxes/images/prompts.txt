UFC-01 COMIC COMPANION — IMAGE PROMPTS
======================================
Page: ufc-01-comic.html
Save all images to the images/ folder next to this file.
The page degrades gracefully — any missing image shows its placeholder + prompt.

GLOBAL STYLE (prepend or keep consistent across all panels):
Comic book style, bold ink outlines, halftone shading. Colour palette,
mood, and lighting are the artist's choice. Consistent characters across
panels:
- ROCCO: young athletic fighter, late 20s, 5'10", grey hoodie, hand wraps.
- COACH: 58, grizzled ex-fighter, scar over right eye, calm bearing, marker in hand.
- MICK: 42, huge heavyweight, military bearing, six inches taller than Rocco.
- DOC: mid-40s woman, sharp-eyed, precise, spiral notebook, coffee mug.

TEXT IS WELCOME. Render text elements naturally in a comic style —
speech bubbles, thought bubbles, caption boxes, sound effects
(SKRRT, BAM, THUD), signage (HALLORAN'S, BARRED), whiteboard writing
(AUTHORIZE, FGT, FAZ, LOGS), and any labels called out in a panel
prompt should actually appear in the image. Hand-lettered, comic-book
style. No vendor logos.

PANEL 02 — THE BOARD (four empty boxes)
Save to: p02-whiteboard-empty.png
Prompt:
Comic book interior panel, bold ink lines, halftone shading, navy/cream/amber
palette. A cluttered boxing gym office above a fight floor: a 58-year-old
grizzled ex-fighter coach with a scar over his right eye stands at a large
whiteboard holding a marker. The whiteboard shows exactly FOUR large empty
rectangles in a row connected by THREE bold arrows pointing left to right,
nothing else. A young fighter in a grey hoodie sits backwards on a folding
chair looking sceptical. In the background a sharp-eyed woman in her 40s sits
at a round table with a spiral notebook and a chipped coffee mug.
Floor-to-ceiling shelf of VHS tapes on the far wall, single window looking
down onto a gym floor. Warm lamp light against grey rainy windows.

PANEL 07 — ENFORCEMENT (nobody gets stopped by a list)
Save to: p07-enforcement.png
Prompt:
Comic book two-beat panel divided by a jagged vertical gutter, bold inks,
halftone. LEFT BEAT tinted cold blue: the barred-list sheet hangs behind an
empty bar, pristine and ignored, while in the background a shadowy figure

Rain outside, noir mood. No readable text.
