The Four Empty Boxes

GLOBAL STYLE (prepend or keep consistent across all panels):
Comic book style, bold ink outlines, halftone shading. Colour palette,
mood, and lighting are the artist's choice. Consistent characters across
panels:
- ROCCO: 28, athletic rookie fighter, 5'10", grey hoodie, hand wraps.
- COACH: 58, grizzled ex-fighter, scar over right eye, calm, marker in hand.
- MICK: 42, huge heavyweight, military bearing, six inches taller than Rocco.
- DOC: 46, sharp-eyed woman, spiral notebook, coffee mug.

TEXT IS WELCOME. Render text elements naturally in a comic style —
speech bubbles, thought bubbles, caption boxes, sound effects
(SKRRT, BAM, THUD), signage (HALLORAN'S, BARRED), whiteboard writing
(AUTHORIZE, FGT, FAZ, LOGS), and any labels called out in a panel
prompt should actually appear in the image. Hand-lettered, comic-book
style. No vendor logos.
