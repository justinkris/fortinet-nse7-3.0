The Camp

Halloran's Gym

An old brick fire station on the edge of town. Downstairs: heavy bags, an octagon cage, a weight room. Upstairs: Coach's office, a wall of fight tapes, and a whiteboard that never gets erased. Seven weeks of camp. Five sessions a week, Monday to Friday. Saturdays are film study with Doc. Sundays are rest.

The Fight

UFC Round 1 at Xperts

Fortinet Ultimate Fabric Challenge Round 1. Seven scoring domains: Security Fabric, CISO Office, NOC, SOC, FortiDLP, OT detection, OT segmentation. Rocco's first shot at Xperts. The clock is real. The scoring is real. The judges are Fortinet.

The Style

Socratic, evidence-first

One question at a time. Prediction before explanation. Hints before answers. Configuration only after the mental model is stable. Coach maps mechanics to fight plans; Mick pressures on defence; Doc produces evidence. The learner reasons more than he reads.
