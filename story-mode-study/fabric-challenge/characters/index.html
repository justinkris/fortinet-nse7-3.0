Rocco “Rocc” Delvecchio
28 · Rookie · First shot at Xperts

Grew up above his uncle’s pizza joint, learned to fight in a converted garage gym before Coach took him in. Technical and hungry, but throws before he reads. Chases finishes when he should be checking evidence. Never quite says “I don’t know” — says walk me through it again.

Growth arc: learning that a match won on evidence is worth more than a match won on speed.

Head CoachEx-Champion
Coach Marcus “Mac” Halloran
58 · Won UFC Round 1 himself, back in his day

Took a bad cut over his right eye that never healed properly and was forced to retire. Bought the old fire station with the last of his fight money and turned it into Halloran’s Gym. Calm, patient, never raises his voice. Never repeats himself either. Knows every trap in Round 1 because he fell into every one of them at Xperts 2001.

Slow it down, Rocc. A network is just a fight plan you draw before the bell.
Known for: never erasing his arrows on the whiteboard. If Coach draws it, it stays.

Sparring PartnerEx-Military
Big Mick O’Sullivan
42 · Six inches taller than Rocco, forty pounds heavier

Did a decade in the infantry before he came home and started fighting. Now he spars for Coach and works security at a pub three nights a week. Plays every opponent Rocco might face. Loves segmentation, defence, and slow methodical pressure. Won’t let Rocco advance a position until Rocco can defend it. Calls Rocco “kid” — never “Rocc”.

Kid. Zones. Then relationships. In that order.
Style: never sarcastic. Just direct. If Rocco does something stupid, Mick lets Rocco figure out why.

CornerwomanFilm Analyst
Doc Silver
46 · Cutman · The sharpest person in the gym

Started as a physio, moved into corner work, now runs film study and between-round strategy for every fighter Coach trains. Reads logs the way she reads a fighter’s footwork — for patterns other people miss. Quiet, precise, speaks only when she has evidence. Was the corner for Coach’s championship fight. Was also the corner the night he got cut. She has never forgiven herself for missing the setup.

Show me the log. One event is a clue. Ordered events are a story.
Also: the only person in the gym who trusts the office coffee machine.
