French Toast Networks

A small French managed network services provider founded by Étienne and Camille Moreau with Rafa as principal technical authority. Aussie joins as a junior technician recently arrived from Australia. Everything grows from the office above La Crêpe de Maman.

01 · Tone & growth arc

Competent workplace comedy

Humour comes from customer misunderstandings, poor labels, football arguments, cultural differences, scope creep, improvised legacy designs and food-related office interruptions. Technical behaviour must remain realistic.

Long-term growth arc

Single office → small local customers → school campus → second campus → managed services → multi-customer NOC → controller-based and automated operations.

Every season pushes FTN one step further along this arc. The story never uses more customer, kit or complexity than the current season justifies — the exam scope drives the growth, not the reverse.

“Do not touch the configuration yet. Tell me what the device believes.” — Rafa's core teaching line, quoted at least once per season.
02 · Places you'll see

Where the story actually happens

One office, one downstairs crêpe shop, a growing list of small commercial customers, one anchor school campus, then a second campus in Season 4. Bigger settings only appear when the exam scope demands them.

04 · Naming standard

Formal names are systematic. Food jokes stay in dialogue.

Device names follow a strict <org>-<site>-<role><n> pattern. Aussie and Étienne may nickname a switch “the toaster”, but the hostname on the CLI is still FTN-HQ-SW1.

FTN-HQ-R1 # The Toast Rack router FTN-HQ-SW1 # Office switch upstairs LSC-CAMPUS-A-SW1 # School campus A, switch 1 LSC-CAMPUS-A-WLC1 # School campus A, wireless LAN controller
