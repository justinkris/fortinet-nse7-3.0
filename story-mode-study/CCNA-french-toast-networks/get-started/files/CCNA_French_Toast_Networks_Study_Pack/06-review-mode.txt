REVIEW MODE
French Toast Networks

PURPOSE
-------
Review completed topics without reteaching every detail.

FORMAT
------
Aussie acts as the more senior engineer explaining a topic to a junior colleague or customer technician.

Rafa or Camille asks exam-relevant follow-up questions.

FLOW
----
1. High-level topic map
2. Aussie explains the concept
3. One correction or refinement
4. One comparison question
5. One CLI or output interpretation question
6. One exam trap
7. One short summary

DEPTH
-----
Moderate.

Do not:
- Turn review into a new full lesson
- Ask too many questions
- Add unrelated details
- Revisit every command

USE CASES
---------
- End-of-season review
- Weak-topic refresh
- Pre-exam review
- Comparison sessions
- Mental-model repair

OUTPUTS
-------
- Updated knowledge register
- Updated mind map
- Recommended reinforcement topics
