CHARACTERS
French Toast Networks

==================================================
AUSSIE
==================================================
ROLE:
Junior Network Technician and learner protagonist

BACKGROUND:
Recently migrated from Australia to France.

STARTING KNOWLEDGE:
Already works in networking and understands many practical concepts, but has uneven foundations and CCNA-specific gaps.

STRENGTHS:
- Practical troubleshooting instincts
- Willingness to ask questions
- Learns quickly with a clear mental model
- Notices details others overlook
- Can connect new ideas to real-world experience

WEAKNESSES:
- Cisco IOS confidence
- Subnetting speed
- STP detail
- OSPF mechanics
- Wireless architecture
- Precise exam terminology

PERSONALITY:
Curious, grounded, friendly, and enthusiastic about France.

LANGUAGE:
Basic French phrases only.
Often misses slang and rapid customer conversations.

RECURRING JOKES:
- Calls football “soccer”
- Tries to explain rugby league
- Uses Australian expressions that confuse the team
- Is delighted by French food
- Occasionally answers a precise exam question with a practical but incomplete answer

GROWTH:
Moves from a capable but uneven technician to an engineer who can explain, verify, and troubleshoot confidently.

==================================================
ÉTIENNE MOREAU
==================================================
ROLE:
Technical Co-Founder and Solutions Architect

STRENGTHS:
- Routing and switching
- Architecture
- Physical infrastructure
- Customer presentations
- Network design

WEAKNESSES:
- Scope control
- Budget realism
- Over-designing small jobs

PERSONALITY:
Warm, expressive, theatrical, and ambitious.

RECURRING JOKES:
- Draws topologies on café napkins
- Defends French football passionately
- Teaches Aussie questionable slang
- Is embarrassed when his mother calls him “mon amour”

DIALOGUE STYLE:
Energetic, confident, occasionally dramatic.

==================================================
CAMILLE MOREAU
==================================================
ROLE:
Co-Founder and Operations Director

STRENGTHS:
- Wireless
- Security
- Operational standards
- Documentation
- Change control
- Automation and programmability
- Requirement clarification

PERSONALITY:
Sharp, composed, dryly funny, and practical.

STORY FUNCTION:
- Translates customer needs into technical requirements
- Prevents unnecessary complexity
- Tests whether a design can actually be delivered
- Reinforces documentation and security

DIALOGUE STYLE:
Concise, precise, often deadpan.

==================================================
RAFAEL “RAFA” COSTA
==================================================
ROLE:
Lead Network Engineer and Technical Authority

NATIONALITY:
Brazilian

STRENGTHS:
- Packet-path reasoning
- Troubleshooting
- Cisco IOS
- Routing protocols
- Subnetting
- Exam interpretation
- Explaining difficult concepts simply

MENTOR STYLE:
Mostly Socratic.
Calm and precise.
Uses humour, hints, and evidence.

CORE TEACHING LINE:
“Do not touch the configuration yet. Tell me what the device believes.”

PERSONALITY:
Relaxed and playful until a real outage occurs, then extremely methodical.

RECURRING JOKES:
- Practises capoeira in the office
- Claims Brazilian football superiority
- Joins Étienne in objecting when Aussie says “soccer”
- Occasionally knocks over cardboard boxes, never equipment

DIALOGUE STYLE:
Short questions, patient hints, clear explanations.

==================================================
MADAME COLETTE MOREAU
==================================================
ROLE:
Owner of La Crêpe de Maman
Étienne’s mother
Recurring supporting character

PERSONALITY:
Warm, practical, direct, and impossible to ignore.

RECURRING BEHAVIOUR:
- Calls Étienne “mon amour”
- Sends food upstairs during incidents
- Describes faults in nontechnical language
- Treats the payment terminal as critical infrastructure
- Knows surprising details about the building
- Tests whether technical explanations make sense to a normal customer

DIALOGUE STYLE:
Practical, affectionate, and impatient with jargon.

==================================================
CAST BOUNDARIES
==================================================
- Aussie is inexperienced in some areas, not incompetent.
- Rafa may joke, but never gives intentionally misleading technical explanations.
- Étienne and Camille’s banter is affectionate, not hostile.
- French and Brazilian traits should add flavour without becoming caricatures.
- French phrases should remain short and understandable from context.
- Technical accuracy takes priority over comedy.
