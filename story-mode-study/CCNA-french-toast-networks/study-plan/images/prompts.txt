CCNA · French Toast Networks — Image Prompts
==============================================================================

One prompt per episode. Each PNG is referenced by study-plan/index.html.

STYLE (applies to every image):
  Flat cartoon illustration, detailed but visually simple. Colourful using a light, gentle palette — soft pastels and light saturated tones (pale sky blue, mint, peach, buttercream, dusty rose, sage, lavender, warm ochre) chosen to suit the scene rather than a fixed theme. No dominant single-colour wash; use several light colours together to keep the image cheerful without being loud. Clean thin dark outlines, flat solid fills, minimal or no shading. No gradients, no cross-hatching, no photorealism, no painterly texture, no neon, no heavy saturation. Small charming details are welcome; noise and grit are not. Characters, key props and one clear focal action should be recognisable at small size. 16:9 landscape composition. Slight competent-workplace-comedy tone (small French managed-services company), but the technical elements should still be recognisable. No vendor logos, no readable brand names on hardware.

CAST REFERENCE (draw whoever is named in the SCENE below):
  Aussie — late-20s Australian junior network technician recently arrived in France, backpack with a small kangaroo pin, laptop covered in stickers, curious and grounded, occasionally delighted by whatever pastry is on the desk.
  Étienne Moreau — warm expressive French co-founder in his late 30s, sleeves rolled up, often mid-sketch on a café napkin, ambitious and theatrical.
  Camille Moreau — sharp composed French co-founder in her late 30s, tidy blazer or cardigan, holding a clipboard or notebook, dryly funny and precise.
  Rafael 'Rafa' Costa — relaxed Brazilian lead engineer in his 40s, casual outfit, sometimes with a small capoeira pose, calm and methodical.
  Madame Colette Moreau — warm practical French woman in her 60s, apron over a blouse, owner of La Crêpe de Maman, often carrying a plate of food upstairs to the engineers.

SETTING REFERENCE:
  The Toast Rack is a small office on the first floor above La Crêpe de Maman, a Nutella crêpe shop in a small French town. The office is a couple of desks with monitors, a shared whiteboard, cardboard boxes of network kit, a coffee corner, and a window overlooking the street. La Crêpe de Maman downstairs has the payment terminal, guest and staff Wi-Fi, a kitchen printer, an office computer, CCTV and a small managed switch. Later Le School de Croissant appears — a modest French school campus large enough to need VLANs, redundancy, wireless and documentation.
